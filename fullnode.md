# Full Node

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

Na computer wey dey download the entire Bitcoin blockchain and e dey independently check every transaction and block wey dey against Bitcoin rules, without trusting anybody else to do am.

## Analogy

Think of community wey dey keep their own copy of village land registry. Instead of asking government office every time somebody wan confirm who own one plot, the community dey check their own records. Full node na that community record-keeper, e no dey trust banks, governments, or other computers. E dey check everything by itself, from the very first Bitcoin transaction to the latest one.

## In Context

If you dey run full node, you no need to trust anybody, you dey verify everything yourself.

When you dey use mobile wallet or exchange, you dey trust them to tell you the truth about your balance and Bitcoin rules. Full node no dey trust anybody, e get e own complete copy of the blockchain and e dey enforce the rules independently. Na the most sovereign way to use Bitcoin.

## Why It Matters

Full nodes na the backbone of Bitcoin decentralisation. The more people wey dey run them across the world, the harder e go be for anybody - governments, miners, developers - to change Bitcoin rules without broad agreement. You no need to run one to use Bitcoin, but the network dey healthier when more people dey do am.
