# Block Explorer

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

Na public website wey dey let anybody search and view every transaction, block, and wallet address wey dey the Bitcoin blockchain - e be like search engine for the entire history of Bitcoin.

## Analogy

Think of the blockchain as public government record - every transaction wey dem ever record dey printed there for anybody to read. Block explorer na the index wey dey help you search am. Instead make you flip through thousands of pages, you go type transaction ID or wallet address and e go immediately show you everything: when e happen, how much dem send, wetin fee dem pay, and how many confirmations e get.

## In Context

I paste the transaction ID for block explorer and I see say e still dey unconfirmed.

After you send Bitcoin, you fit copy your transaction ID (na long string of letters and numbers) and paste am into block explorer like mempool.space or blockstream.info. E go show you exactly where your transaction dey, whether e still dey wait inside mempool, how many blocks don confirm am, and how much fee dem pay.

## Why It Matters

Block explorers dey make Bitcoin transparency real and accessible. Anybody fit verify any transaction without needing special software or technical knowledge. This one dey important for trust: you no need to believe sender say dem make payment, you fit check am yourself. E also mean say Bitcoin no get hidden transactions; everything wey dey on-chain dey visible to the entire world.
