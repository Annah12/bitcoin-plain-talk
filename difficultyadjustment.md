# Difficulty Adjustment

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

Na automatic mechanism wey dey keep Bitcoin block times close to 10 minutes, by making the mining puzzle harder when more miners join the network, and easier when miners dey leave

## Analogy

Imagine school exam where the pass mark dey adjust based on how many students dey sit am. If one thousand brilliant students join, the pass mark go go up so that na only the right number pass. If most students drop out, the pass mark go go down so the class still fit graduate on time. Bitcoin dey do this every 2,016 blocks - roughly every two weeks - e dey automatically recalibrate how hard the mining puzzle be based on how much computing power dey on the network.

## In Context

After the last halving, some miners shut down their machines, e trigger difficulty adjustment downward.

When mining become less profitable, for example after halving cut the block reward, some miners go go off. With fewer miners wey dey compete, blocks go arrive too slowly. Bitcoin go automatically detect this and reduce the mining difficulty so that the 10-minute rhythm go restore.

## Why It Matters

Difficulty adjustment na wetin make Bitcoin reliable. No matter how many miners join or leave, Bitcoin dey keep producing blocks at steady pace. E mean say Bitcoin no fit dey overwhelmed by sudden surge of miners, and e no fit slow to halt if miners leave. Na the self-balancing heartbeat of the network.
