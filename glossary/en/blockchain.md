# Blockchain

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A public record book of every Bitcoin transaction ever made, shared by thousands of computers around the world so no single person controls it.

## Analogy

Imagine a village ledger kept in the marketplace for everyone to see. Every time money changes hands, it's written in the ledger — and everyone has a copy. No one can secretly erase or change an entry because everyone else's copy would disagree. The blockchain is that village ledger, but shared across the entire world.

## In Context

*"The transaction is recorded on the blockchain."*

**What this means:** The payment has been confirmed and permanently written into Bitcoin's public record. It cannot be reversed, deleted, or altered by anyone.

## Why It Matters

The blockchain removes the need to trust a bank or government to keep honest records. The record itself is the truth, and anyone can verify it at any time without asking for permission.
