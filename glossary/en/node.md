# Node

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

A computer that keeps a full copy of the Bitcoin blockchain and checks that every transaction and block follows the rules — nodes are what enforce Bitcoin's laws.

## Analogy

Think of nodes as the community elders in a village who all hold a copy of the official record book. When someone claims a transaction happened, they all check their own copies. If even one record is tampered with, the others immediately spot the fraud. The more elders there are, the harder it is for anyone to cheat.

## In Context

*"Running your own node means you don't have to trust anyone else."*

**What this means:** Instead of relying on a third-party service to tell you what's on the blockchain, you verify everything yourself. Your node independently checks that every transaction you receive is valid and follows the rules.

## Why It Matters

Nodes are the backbone of Bitcoin's decentralisation. No single node is in charge. Each one independently enforces the same rules. This means there's no central point to attack, bribe, or shut down — making Bitcoin censorship-resistant.
