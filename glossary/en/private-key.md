# Private Key

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A secret code that proves you own your bitcoin and lets you spend it — whoever holds this key controls the funds.

## Analogy

Think of it as the PIN to your bank account, except there's no bank to call if you forget it, and no one can reset it for you. If someone else gets your private key, they can take everything. If you lose it, you lose access to your bitcoin — permanently.

## In Context

*"Never share your private key with anyone."*

**What this means:** Your private key is the master password to your bitcoin. Sharing it is like handing someone your entire wallet and saying "take what you want." No legitimate service will ever ask for it.

## Why It Matters

Bitcoin has no customer support. There is no bank to call, no fraud department to dispute a transaction. The private key is the only proof of ownership. Keeping it secret and safe is the most important thing in Bitcoin security.
