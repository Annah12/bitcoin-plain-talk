# Mempool

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

A waiting room for Bitcoin transactions that haven't been confirmed yet.

## Analogy

Imagine sending a letter at the post office. Before it gets delivered, it sits in the sorting room waiting for a delivery van to pick it up. The mempool is that sorting room — your transaction waits there until a miner includes it in a block.

## In Context

*"My transaction has been stuck in the mempool for an hour."*

**What this means:** The sender's payment was sent but hasn't been added to the blockchain yet. It's waiting in the queue. Paying a higher fee would have moved it to the front of the line.

## Why It Matters

When the Bitcoin network is busy, thousands of transactions pile up in the mempool. Miners pick the ones with the highest fees first. Knowing this helps you decide how much fee to pay and why your transaction might be slow.
