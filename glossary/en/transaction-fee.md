# Transaction Fee

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

A small payment you include with your Bitcoin transaction to reward miners for adding it to the blockchain — higher fees get confirmed faster.

## Analogy

It's like paying for express delivery versus standard post. If you pay more, your package goes to the front of the queue. Bitcoin miners look at all the pending transactions and pick the highest-paying ones first. If the network is busy and your fee is low, your transaction waits.

## In Context

*"I set a low fee and my transaction took two days to confirm."*

**What this means:** The person chose a small fee, so miners kept skipping it in favour of higher-paying transactions. When the network got less busy, it was eventually picked up and confirmed.

## Why It Matters

Fees ensure miners are incentivised to process transactions even after all 21 million bitcoin are mined. For users, understanding fees helps you decide how much to pay depending on how urgently you need your transaction confirmed.
