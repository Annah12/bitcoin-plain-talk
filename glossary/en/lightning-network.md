# Lightning Network

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

A payment layer built on top of Bitcoin that lets you send tiny amounts of bitcoin instantly and cheaply — without waiting for a block to confirm.

## Analogy

The main Bitcoin blockchain is like a bank wire transfer: secure and final, but it takes time and costs something. Lightning is like keeping a running tab with a friend at a local market — you settle small amounts back and forth quickly, and only record the final total in the official ledger when you're done.

## In Context

*"I bought coffee with bitcoin over Lightning for less than a cent in fees."*

**What this means:** Using the Lightning Network, the person paid a tiny amount instantly and cheaply — something that would be impractical with regular on-chain Bitcoin transactions, which take ~10 minutes and cost more in fees.

## Why It Matters

Bitcoin on the main chain is too slow and expensive for everyday small payments. Lightning solves this by letting people transact instantly with near-zero fees. It makes Bitcoin practical for buying food, airtime, or paying for services in daily life.
