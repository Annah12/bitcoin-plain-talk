# Cold Storage

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

Keeping your Bitcoin private keys on a device that is never connected to the internet — the safest way to store bitcoin you don't plan to spend soon.

## Analogy

Think of it like burying cash in a watertight container in your backyard versus keeping it in your pocket. What's buried is harder to steal because a thief would have to physically know where to dig. Cold storage means your keys are never online, so hackers have no way to reach them remotely.

## In Context

*"I keep most of my bitcoin in cold storage and only a small amount in a hot wallet for daily use."*

**What this means:** The person stores the bulk of their savings on a hardware device or offline paper wallet that's never connected to the internet. They keep only a small amount in an app on their phone for easy access when needed.

## Why It Matters

The biggest risk to bitcoin savings is online theft. Cold storage removes that risk entirely — if your keys are never online, they can't be hacked online. For anyone holding significant amounts, cold storage is considered essential security.
