# Wallet

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

An app or device that holds your Bitcoin keys and lets you send and receive bitcoin — but it doesn't actually store coins, it stores the keys that prove you own them.

## Analogy

A Bitcoin wallet is less like a physical wallet and more like a keychain. Your bitcoin lives on the blockchain; the wallet holds the keys that unlock it. Losing your keychain doesn't mean the house disappears — but without the keys, you can't get in.

## In Context

*"I moved my bitcoin off the exchange and into my own wallet."*

**What this means:** Instead of leaving bitcoin under a company's control (like a bank), the person moved it to a wallet where only they hold the keys. This means only they can access or send those funds.

## Why It Matters

Keeping bitcoin on an exchange means trusting that company. If they get hacked, go bankrupt, or freeze your account, you lose access. Holding your own wallet means "not your keys, not your coins" — but with that power comes the responsibility of keeping your keys safe.
