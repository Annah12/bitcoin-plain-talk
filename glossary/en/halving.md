# Halving

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

An event that happens approximately every four years where the reward miners earn for adding a new block is cut in half — permanently reducing the rate at which new bitcoin enters circulation.

## Analogy

Imagine a gold mine that produces 50 nuggets a day. After four years, it suddenly starts producing only 25. Four years later, 12.5. It keeps halving until the mine is nearly empty. Bitcoin works the same way — the "mine" has a fixed schedule built into the code, and everyone in the world can see exactly when the next halving will happen.

## In Context

*"The 2024 halving reduced the block reward from 6.25 to 3.125 bitcoin."*

**What this means:** From April 2024, miners now earn 3.125 bitcoin for every block they mine, instead of 6.25. This means new bitcoin is being created at exactly half the previous rate, making it scarcer over time.

## Why It Matters

Halvings are how Bitcoin enforces its fixed supply of 21 million coins. By programmatically reducing issuance, the system ensures no one can inflate bitcoin's supply — unlike a government that can print more money. Historically, halvings have preceded significant price appreciation as supply decreases while demand grows.
