# Public Key

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A code derived from your private key that you can safely share with others — it's used to generate your Bitcoin address so people can send you money.

## Analogy

Think of it like your postal address. You can give it to anyone so they can send you letters. Knowing your address doesn't let someone take your mail or enter your house — for that they'd need your key (the private key).

## In Context

*"Share your public key to receive a payment."*

**What this means:** The person is telling someone to use their public key (or the address derived from it) to send bitcoin. It's safe to share — it only allows others to send to you, not to spend your funds.

## Why It Matters

Understanding the difference between public and private keys is the foundation of Bitcoin security. One can be freely shared; the other must stay secret. Mixing them up is the most common way people lose bitcoin to scams.
