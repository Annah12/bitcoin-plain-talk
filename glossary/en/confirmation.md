# Confirmation

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

A confirmation happens each time a new block is added to the blockchain on top of the block containing your transaction — the more confirmations, the more settled and irreversible your payment is.

## Analogy

Imagine wet cement on a path. Right after pouring, it's still soft — someone could still disturb it. After an hour it starts hardening. After a day it's solid. Bitcoin confirmations work the same way: each new block added on top of yours is another layer of cement hardening the transaction into permanence.

## In Context

*"Wait for at least 6 confirmations before releasing the goods."*

**What this means:** The seller wants to see 6 blocks stacked on top of the payment transaction before trusting that it's final. Six confirmations means roughly 60 minutes have passed and the cost of reversing the transaction is astronomically high.

## Why It Matters

Knowing how confirmations work helps you understand when a payment is truly final. For small everyday purchases, 1-2 confirmations may be fine. For large transfers, waiting for 6 or more is safer and is the standard used by most exchanges and services.
