# Proof of Work

**Category:** Mining

## Plain English

The system Bitcoin uses to make adding blocks to the blockchain expensive and difficult — computers must do real, costly computation to earn the right to record transactions.

## Analogy

Imagine to cast a vote in a village meeting, each person must first solve a hard maths puzzle by hand. It takes effort and time. This stops anyone from casting millions of fake votes — each one would require genuine work. Proof of Work is Bitcoin's version of this: each block requires provable computational effort before it's accepted.

## In Context

*"Proof of Work makes it economically impossible to rewrite Bitcoin's history."*

**What this means:** To change a past transaction on the blockchain, an attacker would need to redo all the computational work for that block AND every block added since — while simultaneously outpacing the entire honest network. The energy cost makes it practically impossible.

## Why It Matters

Proof of Work is what makes Bitcoin trustworthy without a central authority. You don't have to trust any person or company — you trust the math and the energy cost. Changing the rules would require overpowering the entire world's worth of mining hardware.
