# Hashrate

**Category:** Mining

## Plain English

The total computing power being used to secure the Bitcoin network — the higher it is, the harder it is for anyone to attack Bitcoin.

## Analogy

Imagine a lottery where to win, you must solve a very hard puzzle as fast as possible. The hashrate is like counting how many puzzle attempts are being made every second across the entire world. More attempts per second means more security — it becomes almost impossible for one person to cheat.

## In Context

*"Bitcoin's hashrate hit a new all-time high this week."*

**What this means:** More computing machines than ever before are working to secure the Bitcoin network. This is generally a positive sign — it means the network is more secure and more miners believe Bitcoin is worth protecting.

## Why It Matters

A high hashrate means Bitcoin is extremely difficult to attack. To rewrite transaction history, an attacker would need more computing power than the rest of the network combined. The higher the hashrate, the more expensive and practically impossible that becomes.
