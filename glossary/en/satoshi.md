# Satoshi

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

The smallest unit of bitcoin — one bitcoin equals 100 million satoshis, making it possible to own or send a tiny fraction of a bitcoin.

## Analogy

A satoshi is to bitcoin what a cent is to a dollar — except the fractions are much finer. If one bitcoin is worth a lot, satoshis let you deal in small, affordable amounts. You don't need to buy a whole bitcoin any more than you need to buy a whole kilogram of gold.

## In Context

*"I sent 5,000 satoshis as a tip."*

**What this means:** The person sent 0.00005 bitcoin — a very small amount, suitable for a tip or micropayment. At any bitcoin price, satoshis make it easy to send exact small values.

## Why It Matters

Many people assume they can't afford bitcoin because the price per coin seems high. Satoshis remove that barrier — you can buy, save, and send any amount, no matter how small. It's how bitcoin becomes accessible to everyone.
