# UTXO

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

Unspent Transaction Output — the individual pieces of bitcoin you own and can spend, like separate coins in a jar.

## Analogy

Think of your bitcoin not as a single balance but as a collection of different banknotes. If someone sends you 0.03 BTC and another person sends you 0.01 BTC, you don't have one "0.04 BTC" — you have two separate notes. Each of those notes is a UTXO. When you spend, you combine notes and get change back.

## In Context

*"My wallet is consolidating UTXOs to reduce future fees."*

**What this means:** The wallet is combining many small pieces of bitcoin into fewer, larger ones. This makes future transactions cheaper, because fewer UTXOs means a smaller transaction size on the blockchain.

## Why It Matters

Your wallet balance is the sum of all your UTXOs. Understanding this explains why sometimes you get "change" back to yourself when sending, and why wallets with many small UTXOs pay higher fees.
