# Block

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

A group of confirmed Bitcoin transactions bundled together and permanently added to the blockchain approximately every 10 minutes.

## Analogy

Imagine a page in a village record book. Every 10 minutes, a new page is completed, stamped, and glued into the book forever. Each page (block) contains a list of all the transactions that happened in that time. Once glued in, nothing on that page can be changed.

## In Context

*"Your transaction has 3 block confirmations."*

**What this means:** The transaction was included in a block, and 2 more blocks have been added on top of it since. Each new block makes it harder and harder to reverse. After 6 confirmations, it's considered irreversible by most services.

## Why It Matters

Blocks are how Bitcoin reaches agreement. Once a transaction is in a block — and especially once several blocks are stacked on top — it's permanently settled. No bank approval needed, no reversal possible.
