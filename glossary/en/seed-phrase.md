# Seed Phrase

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

A list of 12 or 24 ordinary words that can restore your entire Bitcoin wallet — your ultimate backup if your device is lost, stolen, or broken.

## Analogy

Think of it as the master key to your house, hidden under a stone in your garden. If you lose your regular keys (your device), you can use the master key to get back in. But if someone else finds that stone, they can walk right in too. Your seed phrase works the same way — whoever has it, has your bitcoin.

## In Context

*"Write down your seed phrase and store it somewhere safe offline."*

**What this means:** The person should physically write the 12 or 24 words on paper (never take a screenshot or type it online) and keep it somewhere secure — like a safe or a trusted place known only to them.

## Why It Matters

Your seed phrase is the only way to recover your wallet if something goes wrong with your device. Losing it means permanently losing access to your bitcoin. But it's also the biggest security risk — if stored carelessly, anyone who finds it can take everything without leaving a trace.
