# Address

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

A string of letters and numbers that acts as your Bitcoin receiving account number — you share it with someone so they can send you bitcoin.

## Analogy

It's like your mobile money number. You give someone your M-Pesa or MTN number, they send funds, and the money arrives in your account. A Bitcoin address works the same way — share it, and bitcoin sent to it lands in your wallet.

## In Context

*"Send the payment to this address: bc1qxy2k..."*

**What this means:** The person is sharing their Bitcoin address so the sender knows exactly where to send the funds. The long string of characters is how the Bitcoin network identifies where to deliver the transaction.

## Why It Matters

Addresses let you receive bitcoin from anyone in the world without revealing your identity or your private key. Modern wallets generate a fresh address for each transaction, improving privacy by making it harder to track your full payment history.
