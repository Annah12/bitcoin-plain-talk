# Decentralisation

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

The property of Bitcoin that means no single person, company, or government is in charge — thousands of independent computers around the world maintain the system together.

## Analogy

A traditional bank is like a single water tower supplying a whole city. If the tower is damaged or shut down, no one gets water. Bitcoin is like a network of thousands of independent wells scattered across the country — you can block some, but you can never stop them all.

## In Context

*"Bitcoin's decentralisation makes it resistant to government shutdown."*

**What this means:** Because Bitcoin runs on thousands of computers in different countries, no single authority can turn it off. Even if a government bans Bitcoin in one country, the network continues running through nodes in every other country.

## Why It Matters

Decentralisation is what gives Bitcoin its core property: no one can change the rules, freeze your funds, or shut it down. For people in countries with unstable governments or financial systems, this is especially important — your money is yours, protected by math, not by the goodwill of an institution.
