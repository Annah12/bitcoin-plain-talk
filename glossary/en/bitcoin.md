# Bitcoin

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

A digital form of money that no government or company controls — you can send it to anyone in the world, directly, without a bank in the middle.

## Analogy

Think of gold: it has value because it's scarce, portable, and people around the world agree it's worth something. Bitcoin works the same way, except it's digital. There will only ever be 21 million bitcoin — built into the rules of the system — so no government can print more of it and inflate its value away.

## In Context

*"I sent 0.001 bitcoin to pay for the service."*

**What this means:** A small amount of bitcoin (about 100,000 satoshis) was sent directly from the sender's wallet to the recipient. No bank approved it. No borders slowed it down.

## Why It Matters

For people in countries with unstable currencies or limited banking access, Bitcoin offers a way to save and send money that no one can confiscate, freeze, or inflate away. It's permission-less: anyone with a phone can use it.
