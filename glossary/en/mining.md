# Mining

**Category:** Mining

## Plain English

The process where powerful computers compete to solve a puzzle, and the winner gets to add the next block of transactions to the blockchain and earn newly created bitcoin as a reward.

## Analogy

Imagine a competition where thousands of people are each rolling a huge set of dice, racing to be the first to roll a specific combination. The winner records the next page in the village ledger and gets paid for doing so. Bitcoin mining is that competition — except computers roll the dice billions of times per second.

## In Context

*"Mining bitcoin in Africa is becoming more viable with cheaper solar energy."*

**What this means:** As the cost of electricity (the main cost of mining) drops — for example, through solar panels — it becomes more profitable to run the computers that secure the Bitcoin network and earn mining rewards.

## Why It Matters

Mining is what makes Bitcoin secure without a central authority. No company or government adds new blocks — computers around the world compete fairly to do it. Miners are also how new bitcoin enters circulation, following a fixed schedule that ends at 21 million coins.
