# Address

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

Na string of letters and numbers wey work like your Bitcoin receiving account number — you share am with somebody so dem fit send you bitcoin.

## Analogy

E be like your mobile money number. You give person your number, dem send money, the money enter your account. Bitcoin address dey work same way — share am, and bitcoin wey dem send to am go land for your wallet.

## In Context

*"Send the payment to this address: bc1qxy2k..."*

**What this means:** The person dey share their Bitcoin address so the sender go know exactly where to send the funds. That long string of characters na how Bitcoin network identify where to deliver the transaction.

## Why It Matters

Addresses allow you to receive bitcoin from anybody in the world without revealing your identity or your private key. Modern wallets dey generate fresh address for each transaction, which improve privacy by making am harder to track your full payment history.
