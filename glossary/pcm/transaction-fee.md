# Transaction Fee

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

Na small money wey you add with your Bitcoin transaction to pay miners for adding am to the blockchain — higher fee mean faster confirmation.

## Analogy

E be like choosing between express delivery and normal post. You pay more, your package go front of the queue. Bitcoin miners go look all the pending transactions and pick the ones wey pay highest first. If network busy and your fee small, your transaction go dey wait.

## In Context

*"I set low fee and my transaction take two days to confirm."*

**What this means:** The person choose small fee, so miners keep skipping their transaction for ones wey dey pay more. When network become less busy, dem finally pick am and confirm am.

## Why It Matters

Fees make sure miners get reason to process transactions even after all 21 million bitcoin don mine finish. For users, to understand fees help you decide how much to pay depending on how urgent you need your transaction to confirm.
