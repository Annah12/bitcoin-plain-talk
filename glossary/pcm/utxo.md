# UTXO

**Category:** Transactions

## Plain English

Unspent Transaction Output — na the separate pieces of bitcoin wey you get and fit spend, like different notes inside your wallet.

## Analogy

Think your bitcoin no be one balance but like different notes wey you gather. If person send you 0.03 BTC and another person send 0.01 BTC, you no get "0.04 BTC one one" — you get two separate notes. Each of those notes na UTXO. When you wan spend, you combine notes and collect change back.

## In Context

*"My wallet dey consolidate UTXOs to reduce future fees."*

**What this means:** The wallet dey combine plenty small-small pieces of bitcoin into fewer, bigger ones. This one go make future transactions cheaper because fewer UTXOs means smaller transaction size for the blockchain.

## Why It Matters

Your wallet balance na the sum of all your UTXOs. When you understand this, you go sabi why sometimes you dey receive "change" back to yourself when you dey send, and why wallets wey get plenty small UTXOs dey pay higher fees.
