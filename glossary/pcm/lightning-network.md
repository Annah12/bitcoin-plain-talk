# Lightning Network

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

Na payment layer wey dem build on top of Bitcoin wey allow you to send small small bitcoin immediately and cheap — without waiting for block to confirm.

## Analogy

Main Bitcoin blockchain be like bank wire transfer — e dey secure and final, but e dey take time and e dey cost something. Lightning be like keeping running tab with your friend for local market — you dey settle small amounts back and forth quickly, and you only record the final total for the official ledger when you don finish.

## In Context

*"I buy coffee with bitcoin through Lightning for less than one kobo for fee."*

**What this means:** Using Lightning Network, the person pay small amount immediately and cheaply — something wey go dey impractical with regular on-chain Bitcoin transactions wey take ~10 minutes and cost more for fees.

## Why It Matters

Bitcoin on the main chain too slow and too expensive for everyday small payments. Lightning solve this by allowing people to transact immediately with near-zero fees. E make Bitcoin practical for buying food, airtime, or paying for daily services.
