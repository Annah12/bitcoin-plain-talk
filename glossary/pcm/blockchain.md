# Blockchain

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Na public record book of every Bitcoin transaction wey ever happen, wey thousands of computers for the world dey share so that no single person dey control am.

## Analogy

Imagine say na community book wey dem keep for market square for everybody to see. Every time money change hand, dem write am for the book — and everybody get their own copy. Nobody fit secretly erase or change anything because everybody else copy go disagree. Blockchain na that community book, but dem share am for the whole world.

## In Context

*"The transaction don record for blockchain."*

**What this means:** The payment don confirm and dem don write am forever for Bitcoin public record. Nobody — not even government or any company — fit reverse, delete, or change am.

## Why It Matters

Blockchain remove the need to trust bank or government to keep honest records. The record itself na the truth, and anybody fit verify am any time without asking anybody for permission.
