# Public Key

**Category:** Security

## Plain English

Na code wey come from your private key wey you fit share with anybody without wahala — dem use am to create your Bitcoin address so people fit send you money.

## Analogy

Think am like your house address or your phone number. You fit give anybody so dem fit send you something. Knowing your address no allow person to collect your things or enter your house — for that dem go need your key (the private key).

## In Context

*"Share your public key to receive payment."*

**What this means:** The person dey tell somebody to use their public key (or the address wey come from am) to send bitcoin. E safe to share — e only allow others to send to you, e no allow them to spend your money.

## Why It Matters

To understand the difference between public and private key na the foundation of Bitcoin security. One fit dey shared freely; the other must stay secret. People wey mix dem up na the most common way dem lose bitcoin to scam.
