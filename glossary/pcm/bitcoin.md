# Bitcoin

**Category:** Economy

## Plain English

Bitcoin na digital money wey no be any government or company get — you fit send am to anybody for this world, straight, without any bank for middle.

## Analogy

Think am like gold wey everybody for world agree say e get value. Bitcoin dey work same way, but e dey online. Only 21 million bitcoin go ever exist — e don enter the code so no government fit print more and spoil the value. Na like say dem lock the amount forever.

## In Context

*"I send 0.001 bitcoin go pay for the job."*

**What this means:** Small small bitcoin (like 100,000 satoshi) go from the person wey send direct to the one wey receive. No bank approve am. No border slow am down.

## Why It Matters

For people wey dey countries where the naira or cedi dey fall, or where bank no dey serve everybody, Bitcoin give person way to save and send money wey nobody fit seize, freeze, or spoil the value. You no need anybody permission — anybody wey get phone fit use am.
