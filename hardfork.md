# Hard Fork

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

Na change to Bitcoin rules wey no dey backwards-compatible, e mean say nodes wey no upgrade go permanently separate from those wey upgrade, e go create two different chains.

## Analogy

Imagine say one road split into two path and e no get way to go back to the original. Cars wey dey old path no fit travel for the new one, and vice versa. Hard fork na that kind split, once e happen, the two sides of the network go dey their own separate ways. Na so dem create Bitcoin Cash for 2017: some people disagree with Bitcoin direction, dem change the rules in a way wey no compatible, and the network con split into two coins.

## In Context

Dem create Bitcoin Cash through hard fork of Bitcoin.

Some developers and miners change Bitcoin rules in way wey the original Bitcoin network no go accept. From that point, both chains continue independently, one as Bitcoin (BTC), one as Bitcoin Cash (BCH). Anybody wey hold Bitcoin before the split go receive equal amount of both coins.

## Why It Matters

Hard fork dey permanent. Once the split happen, the two networks dey completely separate, different coins, different communities, different rules. Na why people dey propose major changes to Bitcoin carefully and e dey take years of discussion. If hard fork happen by accident or people dey fight about am, e fit divide community and cause confusion about which chain na the "real" one.
