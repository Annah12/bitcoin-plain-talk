# SegWit

**Category:** Technology

## Plain English

E short for Segregated Witness. Na 2017 upgrade to Bitcoin wey make transactions smaller and cheaper by reorganising how dem dey store transaction data.

## Analogy

Think of bus wey get assigned seats. Before SegWit, every passenger (transaction) dey carry their luggage for their lap, e dey take up seat space. SegWit say: move all your luggage come the boot of the bus. Suddenly, the same bus fit carry more passengers. Transactions con dey lighter, more fit enter each block, and fees drop as result.

## In Context

Make sure say you dey send to SegWit address to save on fees.

SegWit addresses (wey usually dey start with "bc1") dey use the upgraded transaction format, so dem dey take less space inside block and therefore e dey cost less fees. If you send to legacy address instead, you go pay small more.

## Why It Matters

SegWit na one of Bitcoin most significant upgrades. E fix long-standing technical bug wey dem dey call transaction malleability, e reduce fees, and e lay groundwork for Lightning Network. Dem activate am as soft fork, meaning say nobody force to upgrade, but almost everybody eventually do am because of the benefits.
