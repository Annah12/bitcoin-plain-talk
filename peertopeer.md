# Peer-To-Peer

**Category:** Network

## Plain English

Na system where two people fit transact directly with each other - no bank, no middleman, no need to ask anybody permission.

## Analogy

To send money through bank na like sending parcel through courier company, e go pass through third party wey fit delay am, charge fees, or even refuse to deliver am. Peer-to-peer na like handing that parcel directly to the person. For Lagos, think of the difference between sending money through POS agent versus handing somebody cash directly for market. Bitcoin na the digital version of that direct handoff - anybody, anywhere, fit send to anybody else without begging anybody.

## In Context

Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System.

Na the subtitle of Bitcoin original whitepaper wey Satoshi Nakamoto write for 2008. E dey describe exactly wetin Bitcoin design to be - way for people to send money directly to each other for over the internet, the same way you dey send email, without needing bank for the middle.

## Why It Matters

Peer-to-peer mean say no single company or government dey control who fit send or receive Bitcoin. Person wey dey country without banking access fit receive Bitcoin from anywhere for the world. No need for account, no need for approval. Na one of the most powerful properties of Bitcoin, especially for people for places where financial infrastructure dey limited or unreliable.
